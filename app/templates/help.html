📌 Overview

The Ear Disease Classification System is an AI-powered tool designed to help analyze ear conditions using both otoscopic images and symptom information. The system combines machine learning models to provide comprehensive diagnostic assistance.

⚠️ Important Disclaimer: This system is for educational purposes only and should NOT be used as a substitute for professional medical diagnosis. Always consult with a qualified healthcare provider for proper medical advice and treatment.

🔬 How It Works

1. Image Analysis (Vision Transformer)

The system uses a Vision Transformer (ViT) neural network trained on otoscopic images to analyze the visual characteristics of the ear. It examines features like eardrum appearance, inflammation, and other visual indicators.

2. Symptom Analysis (Random Forest)

A Random Forest classifier analyzes patient symptoms including pain, discharge, hearing loss, fever, and other indicators to provide an independent diagnostic assessment.

3. Fusion (Decision-Level Late Fusion)

The final diagnosis combines predictions from both models using weighted averaging:
Final Diagnosis = 80% × Image Analysis + 20% × Symptom Analysis

🩺 Diseases Classification

Acute Otitis Media

A sudden ear infection characterized by inflammation of the middle ear. Common in children and typically presents with ear pain, fever, and hearing difficulties. Requires prompt medical attention.

Chronic Otitis Media

A long-term ear infection or inflammation of the middle ear. Often results in permanent hearing loss and may involve eardrum perforation. Requires ongoing monitoring and management.

Cerumen Impaction

Excessive buildup of earwax (cerumen) that blocks the ear canal. Can cause temporary hearing loss and discomfort. Usually treatable through professional ear cleaning.

Myringosclerosis

A condition characterized by hardening and scarring of the eardrum, often following previous infections or surgery. Can affect hearing and requires monitoring.

Normal

A healthy ear with no detected abnormalities. The eardrum appears normal, and there are no signs of infection or disease.

📖 How to Use the System

Step 1: Upload Image

  • Provide a clear otoscopic image of the ear
  • Supported formats: JPG, PNG, GIF
  • Maximum file size: 16MB
  • You can drag and drop the image or click to browse

Step 2: Select Symptoms

  • Check all symptoms that apply to the patient
  • Leave unchecked if symptoms are not present
  • Available symptoms: Pain, Discharge, Hearing Loss, Fever, Blocked Ear, Itching, Dizziness, Tinnitus, Long Duration

Step 3: Get Diagnosis

  • Click the "Get Diagnosis" button
  • The system will analyze the image and symptoms
  • Results will be displayed with confidence scores

Step 4: Review Results

  • Image Analysis: Shows diagnosis based on the otoscopic image
  • Symptom Analysis: Shows diagnosis based on reported symptoms
  • Final Diagnosis: Combined result from both analyses
  • Clinical Summary: Interpretation and recommendations

📊 Understanding Results

Confidence Scores

The confidence score (0-100%) indicates how certain the system is about its prediction. Higher scores indicate stronger confidence. However, even high-confidence predictions should be verified by a healthcare professional.

Probability Distribution

The system shows probability scores for all possible diseases. These represent the likelihood of each condition based on the analysis. A clear winner (highest probability) suggests a more definitive diagnosis.
